Tenders & Contract Awards

AIFF runs 148+ tenders/RFPs (see Documents for the full list), but most of those are just the call for bids — AIFF doesn't always publish who won. This page covers the subset where AIFF did publish a result: who bid, who won, and for how much.

⚠️ Read this before the numbers below

  • This is a small, incomplete slice. Of 148+ tenders/RFPs on AIFF's site, only a handful have a published result document at all — most tenders' outcomes simply aren't public. A short list here does not mean AIFF only ran a few tenders.
  • Some tenders bundle multiple line items (e.g. two separate events in one tender) with a separate amount for each. We show every amount found but do not guess which company "won" the tender as a whole when it isn't stated — read the source PDF.
  • Some published "results" list bids without declaring a winner. We only claim "awarded to X" when AIFF's own document explicitly says so (its "L1 bidder" language). Otherwise we show the bid comparison and let you read AIFF's own document for any award decision.
  • These are individual contract values, not AIFF's total spending. See Financials for audited income/expenditure — do not add up this page's numbers and call it "AIFF's budget."
