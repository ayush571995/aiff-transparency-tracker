Why this exists

AIFF is recognised by the Government of India as the National Sports Federation for football and receives government support, which is why its governance and spending are a matter of public interest — comparable to how the CAG, RTI, and public accountability norms apply to publicly funded bodies. This tracker doesn't accuse anyone of anything beyond what a cited, dated source already says. It just makes AIFF's own disclosures — and independent reporting about it — easier to find, search and follow over time.
