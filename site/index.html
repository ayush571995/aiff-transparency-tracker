Why this exists

Every kid who's grown up watching India fail to qualify for a World Cup has heard the same excuses: not enough talent, not enough grassroots investment, not enough time. Less discussed is how the federation entrusted with fixing that is actually run and funded — and whether the people asking fans for patience are holding themselves to the same standard they'd demand of a player on the pitch. AIFF is recognised by the Government of India as the National Sports Federation for football and receives government support, which is why its governance and spending are a matter of public interest — comparable to how the CAG, RTI, and public accountability norms apply to publicly funded bodies (see Conduct & Rules). This tracker doesn't accuse anyone of anything beyond what a cited, dated source already says — it just makes AIFF's own disclosures, and independent reporting about it, easier to find, search, and hold up against the standard AIFF is legally supposed to meet.
