Legal: Editorial Policy, Disclaimer & Rights

This page exists to be read, not skipped. It explains exactly what this site claims, what it doesn't, and how anyone — including AIFF or any individual named here — can get something corrected. This is a plain-language policy, not a substitute for professional legal advice; if you're relying on it for anything high-stakes, get a lawyer to review the live site.

1. What this site is

An independent, non-commercial index of (a) documents AIFF has itself published on its own website, and (b) a hand-curated timeline of governance events reported by mainstream Indian and international sports media. It is run by an individual, not a company, news organisation, or any entity affiliated with AIFF, FIFA, AFC, the Government of India, or any political party.

2. Sourcing standard — the core protection for everyone, including us

  • Every factual claim links to a primary or reputable secondary source. Scraped data (Documents, News, Governance) links to AIFF's own published page or PDF. Timeline and Financials entries link to the specific news article or AIFF-published PDF (with page number where applicable) they're drawn from.
  • Allegations are labelled as allegations. Where a claim is disputed or denied — e.g. the 2024 corruption allegation against AIFF's President — the status field says so explicitly, and the person's denial/response is included alongside the allegation, not omitted.
  • We do not draw conclusions the sources don't support. An auditor's "Disclaimer of Opinion" is reported as exactly that — a disclaimer of opinion, with the auditors' own stated reasons — not translated into an accusation of theft or fraud we have no basis for.
  • No fabricated data, ever. Where AIFF hasn't published something (e.g. month-by-month spending, or FY2025-26 accounts), we say so explicitly rather than estimating, interpolating, or inventing a number. See the note on Financials.

3. Why this is lawful public-interest commentary

Under Indian law, fair and bona fide comment on the public conduct of a public body — and truthful statement of fact for the public good — are recognised defences to defamation (see the "truth" and "public good" exceptions historically under Section 499 IPC, carried forward under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and the common-law "fair comment" doctrine). AIFF is a Government-recognised National Sports Federation, subject to CAG audit and RTI obligations under the National Sports Development Code 2011 and the National Sports Governance Act 2025 (see Conduct & Rules) — its governance and spending are squarely a matter of legitimate public interest, the same category of scrutiny applied to any publicly funded body. This site sticks to (a) reporting what AIFF itself has published, and (b) attributed reporting of what named news outlets have reported, which is the safest possible position within that doctrine.

4. Right of reply

If you are AIFF, an AIFF office bearer, or any individual named on this site and you believe something is inaccurate, missing context, or unfair: contact us via the repository (see below). We will publish a linked response alongside the relevant entry, not just quietly remove it — unless the underlying claim is simply wrong, in which case we correct or remove it and note that a correction was made.

5. Corrections process

Open an issue or pull request on the project's GitHub repository describing the error and, if possible, the correct information with its own source. Corrections are the highest-priority kind of contribution to this project — the entire premise depends on accuracy.

6. Copyright

We do not reproduce AIFF's documents or any publisher's news articles. Documents are linked to AIFF's own hosted PDFs (not mirrored or re-hosted). News entries carry only a title, category, date and a link to the original — no article text is copied. Any images shown (e.g. office bearer photos on the Governance page) are hotlinked from AIFF's own site, not stored or redistributed by us. If a rights holder believes anything here nonetheless infringes, contact us via the repository and it will be reviewed and addressed promptly.

7. No affiliation, no official status

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to AIFF, FIFA, the AFC, SAI, MYAS, or the Government of India in any way. Use of "FIFA" in this project's name is purely descriptive (referring to the actual FIFA World Cup, the subject of India's ambitions) and does not imply any endorsement or affiliation.

8. No warranty

This is a volunteer-run, best-effort index. Scraped data may lag, misparse, or miss items (see known limitations on Documents). Nothing here should be treated as financial, legal, or investment advice, or as a certified/audited record — always verify against the original source before relying on any figure.

9. Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused, open-source analytics tool, to see basic aggregate traffic (page views, referring site, browser/OS, and approximate country derived from IP address, which GoatCounter does not store). It sets no cookies and does not track individuals across sites or build user profiles — it exists only so the maintainer can see whether anyone is reading this. Beyond that and standard hosting-provider access logs (automatically generated by GitHub Pages), this site has no account system and does not sell or share visitor data with anyone.
